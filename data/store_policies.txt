
POLICY_ID: RETURN-001
TITLE: Standard Return Policy
TOPIC: returns
SOURCE: Returns Policy, Section 1
TEXT: Unopened non-perishable products in their original packaging can be returned within 7 days of purchase with a valid receipt. Opened non-defective products are not eligible for a standard return.

---

POLICY_ID: RETURN-002
TITLE: Perishable Product Returns
TOPIC: returns
SOURCE: Returns Policy, Section 2
TEXT: Perishable products cannot be returned unless they are defective. Customers should contact store staff for assistance with defective perishable products.

---

POLICY_ID: REFUND-001
TITLE: Refund Processing Time
TOPIC: refunds
SOURCE: Refund Policy, Section 1
TEXT: Approved refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days.
